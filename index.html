🌱 Hyperspectral Sapling Detection

Detecting young saplings before crown formation using 200-band spectral signatures — a proof-of-concept for ARR monitoring and carbon dMRV

▶ Run in Colab 📦 GitHub Repo

🎯 The Problem

Invisible to RGB Young saplings without a crown have no visible canopy signature in standard imagery.
Sub-pixel at satellite scale At 10–30 m/pixel, a 1m sapling occupies <1% of a pixel — classification misses it entirely.
Distinct spectral fingerprint Woody tissue (lignin, cellulose) absorbs at specific wavelengths that grass does not.
Hyperspectral captures it 200+ bands spanning 400–2500 nm resolve the biochemical differences RGB cannot.

🔬 Key Spectral Discriminators

These wavelength regions separate young woody vegetation from grass and mature trees.

FeatureWavelengthWhy It Matters
Red Edge Position690–740 nmShifts between developmental stages of vegetation
NDRE750 & 705 nm3× more sensitive than NDVI for small green patches
Lignin Index (NDLI)~1754 nmWoody tissue marker — the strongest sapling signal
Cellulose (CAI)2000–2200 nmStructural plant material in stems and bark
Leaf Water~970, ~1200 nmYoung leaves hold more water than mature foliage
Key finding: The "Grass-trees" (sapling proxy) spectral signature sits consistently between Grass-pasture and Woods in the lignin and cellulose absorption regions. This biochemical gradient — herbaceous → young woody → mature woody — is the foundation of hyperspectral sapling detection.

🧪 Sub-Pixel Detection via Spectral Unmixing

Linear unmixing decomposes each pixel into fractional abundances of four endmembers — detecting saplings even when they occupy a small fraction of the pixel.

EndmemberSource ClassRole
Soil / BareStone-Steel-Towers (16)Non-vegetated baseline
GrassGrass-pasture (5)Herbaceous background
Woody SaplingGrass-trees (6)Detection target
Mature CanopyWoods (14)Fully formed trees
Why this matters for ARR: At satellite resolution (10–30 m/pixel), individual saplings are sub-pixel objects. Spectral unmixing can flag a pixel as containing saplings when they occupy as little as 20% of the pixel area — something pixel-level classification cannot do.

🚀 Expansion Plan

From proof-of-concept to production dMRV — a four-phase roadmap.

Phase 1 — Validate
Real Sapling Data Collection
Partner with ARR project sites. Fly drone-mounted hyperspectral sensors over known sapling plots. GPS-tag individual saplings for ground truth. Confirm that lignin/cellulose indices discriminate real pre-crown saplings.
DJI Matrice Headwall Nano RTK GPS
Phase 2 — Temporal
Multi-Temporal Signature Tracking
Monthly/quarterly repeat flights. Track how spectral signatures evolve as saplings grow. Build a temporal confidence score: a pixel that becomes increasingly "woody" over 6–12 months is a confirmed sapling.
Time series Change detection LSTM / Transformer
Phase 3 — Scale
Satellite Integration
Use Sentinel-2 (10 m, 13 bands) as the wide-area screening layer. Confirm detections with PRISMA or EnMAP hyperspectral satellites (30 m, 200+ bands). Spectral unmixing enables sub-pixel detection even at 30 m.
Sentinel-2 PRISMA EnMAP GEE
Phase 4 — Production
dMRV API & VM0047 Integration
Automated sapling density estimation per hectare. REST API: upload HSI tile → get sapling density map + confidence score. Integrate with carbon credit verification workflows under Verra's VM0047 methodology.
REST API VM0047 Cloud GPU GeoTIFF

20 m resolution Individual saplings are sub-pixel Drone HSI (cm-scale) for validation; unmixing for satellite
Single time point No temporal confirmation of growth Phase 2: multi-temporal analysis
